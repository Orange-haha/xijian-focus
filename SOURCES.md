# 素材来源

## 动态视频

以下视频均来自 Mixkit，并按 [Mixkit Stock Video Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#videoFree) 使用：

- [Ocean waves bursting on the shore of the coast](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/ocean-waves-bursting-on-the-shore-of-the-coast-4078/)
- [Raining in a cloud forest full of tall trees](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/raining-in-a-cloud-forest-full-of-tall-trees-22728/)
- [Open environment of a cold and snowy forest](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/open-environment-of-a-cold-and-snowy-forest-3310/)
- [Campfire burning wood logs in the dark](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/campfire-burning-wood-logs-in-the-dark-22730/)
- [Landscape of mountains and sunset](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/landscape-of-mountains-and-sunset-3128/)
- [Night sky with stars at a calm lake time lapse](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/night-sky-with-stars-at-a-calm-lake-time-lapse-1704/)

视频通过 Mixkit CDN 远程加载；连接不可用时，网页自动回退到本地场景封面。

## 场景封面

`public/assets/scenes/` 下的六张场景封面由 OpenAI 图像生成工具为本项目生成，并转换为 WebP 格式。

## 环境音

所有环境音均由浏览器 Web Audio API 实时合成，不包含第三方音频文件。
